### 🧠 What I build

- **📈 Algorithmic trading** — MetaTrader 5 Expert Advisors in MQL5, TradingView strategies in Pine Script, signal copiers and arbitrage scanners across sportsbooks, DeFi and prediction markets
- **🤖 AI agents** — voice assistants that answer real phone calls, multi-agent document pipelines, autonomous sales agents built on LLMs
- **⚙️ Automation tools** — scrapers, data pipelines and small web/mobile apps that remove repetitive work for real businesses
